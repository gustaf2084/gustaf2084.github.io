Privacy Policy — godwing (PayPal App)

Last updated: November 12, 2026

"godwing" is a personal PayPal app. It lets the owner of a PayPal account manage that same account through a personal AI assistant. This policy explains what information the app receives and how it is used.

Information the app receives

When you sign in with PayPal ("Log in with PayPal"), PayPal shares the following information with this app, only with your consent:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your PayPal account ID (payer ID)
  • Your account verification status

The app does not receive your PayPal password, bank account details, or card numbers.

How the information is used

The information is used only to let you manage your own PayPal account: viewing your transaction history, checking balances, and sending payouts, always at your request. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by the assistant platform and are used only to carry out your requests. You can revoke this app's access at any time in your PayPal account settings (Settings > Data & Privacy > Manage your permissions, or by removing the app's connection).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
